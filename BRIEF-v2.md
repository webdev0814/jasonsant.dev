# jasonsant.dev Rebuild Brief

## Project

Rebuild jasonsant.dev as a static GitHub Pages portfolio for an Enterprise AI Solutions Architect and Principal Delivery Leader.

Ship date: Sunday, November 1, 2026.

## Business goal

Convert senior technical recruiters, hiring managers, and enterprise consulting buyers seeking an experienced leader for:

- $110–$160/hr
- C2C through Corporate Consulting LLC, a Wyoming LLC
- W-2 contract engagements

The site should make Jason’s fit, commercial terms, enterprise experience, governance mindset, credentials, and public technical work understandable within seconds.

## Audience priorities

1. Senior recruiters matching Jason to architecture, program leadership, business analysis, and Microsoft data roles.
2. Hiring managers who need a leader able to connect strategy, requirements, architecture, governance, and execution.
3. Enterprise buyers evaluating a governed AI, automation, cloud, or data engagement.

## Positioning

Jason is an Enterprise AI Solutions Architect and Principal Delivery Leader who turns ambiguous transformation goals into governed systems, executable roadmaps, and adopted workflows.

Relevant environments and capabilities include:

- Agentic AI and human-in-the-loop automation
- Azure and Microsoft Fabric
- Salesforce and S/4HANA
- Technical program management and SAFe delivery
- Business analysis and requirements governance
- NYC HPD and state/local government environments
- HIPAA and Section 508 considerations

## ROI calculator

Provide an accessible estimator using:

- Team size
- Weekly manual hours per person
- Blended hourly rate
- Working year fixed at 48 weeks
- Adjustable share of manual hours saved

Calculation:

`team size × weekly manual hours × blended rate × 48 = annual manual cost`

The defaults must produce:

`10 × 5 × $120 × 48 = $288,000`

Also calculate estimated annual hours saved and estimated annual value of hours saved. Clearly state that results are planning estimates, not guaranteed savings.

Requirements:

- Explicit `<label>` elements
- Sensible input limits
- Live range-value feedback
- `aria-live` result output
- Locale-formatted numbers and currency
- No misleading “ROI” ratio when implementation cost is not supplied

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508-aware implementation:

- One `<!doctype html>`
- Semantic heading order and landmarks
- Skip link
- Keyboard-operable mobile navigation
- Accessible tabs
- Explicit calculator labels
- Visible focus indicators
- Sufficient contrast
- Minimum practical target sizing
- No essential information conveyed through color alone
- Reduced-motion support
- Progressive enhancement when JavaScript is unavailable

## Technical constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript
- No framework
- No build step
- No third-party JavaScript
- Keep all primary content available in the HTML
- Do not modify `calendar/`, `.github/`, `scripts/`, or `CNAME`

## Design direction

Direction: executive systems dossier with an architectural signal-line motif.

Mood: authoritative, exact, calm, and technically fluent.

Palette:

- Carbon: `#101820`
- Blueprint: `#123B56`
- Signal cyan: `#00A6A6`
- Electric amber: `#FFB000`
- Paper: `#F4F7F8`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Typography:

- Display: Georgia with high-contrast editorial scale
- Interface and body: “Aptos”, “Segoe UI”, and system sans-serif fallbacks

Layout:

- A left-aligned, asymmetric hero with a compact availability rail
- A horizontal fast-facts ledger
- Alternating light and dark editorial sections
- Case studies as ruled dossiers rather than generic floating cards
- Long-form text constrained to readable line lengths

Signature:

A restrained “governance trace” line runs through the hero and selected section boundaries, visually connecting business intent, architecture, controls, and outcomes.

Avoid:

- Purple gradients
- Generic glass-card grids
- Identical rounded cards
- Decorative metrics
- All-caps eyebrow labels above every section
- Unnecessary motion
- Unsupported social proof
- LinkedIn links until a verified URL is supplied

## Content voice

Use concise, active, enterprise-facing language. Prefer concrete terms such as architecture, controls, requirements, delivery, evidence, and adoption. Avoid inflated claims, vague futurism, and generic AI marketing language.
